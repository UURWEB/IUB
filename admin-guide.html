How to approve a new business

Review the applicationOpen Programs & Economy → Applications. Confirm the name, industry and requested bank.
Approve the entityApproval creates the business and its first operating account under the applicant’s normal login.
Verify the accountOpen Finance Services or Network Admin and check the business account, currency and owner membership.
Add staff only when neededThe owner can grant accountant, payroll, banking, CSE or manager access without sharing a password.

Tax workflow

Review categorized earningsPayroll, invoices/contracts, casino winnings, interest, dividends and realized gains are counted. Ordinary transfers are excluded by default.
Compare the member filingA difference is a reason to investigate—not automatic proof of wrongdoing.
Choose bill or immediate collectionIssue a bill for ordinary collection. Use Collect now only when immediate collection is authorized.
Review treasury and ledgerConfirm that the assessment, payment and treasury entry all exist.

Casino safety checklist

Set the winnings taxTax is withheld from net winnings and recorded in the government ledger.
Set a maximum betKeep it small relative to the loss-protection limit and the number of active players.
Enable treasury protectionThe floor locks when cumulative house losses reach the configured limit.
Review before resettingDo not reset the house ledger merely to hide losses. Adjust odds/limits and document the reason.
