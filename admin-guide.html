How to use the finance network

The system is split into daily administration, government programs, member/business tools and corporate ownership. Use the section below that matches the job you are trying to do.

Daily administrator tasks

Open Network Administration.

  • All Accounts: inspect, freeze or correct a specific bank account.
  • Users & Roles: create users and appoint managers.
  • Loans & Bills: approve loans, see active loans and collect repayments.
  • Treasury & Ledger: move money between institution accounts and search transactions.
  • Casino: protect the house treasury and set casino taxes.
  • Head Admin Support: Minekid123 can temporarily troubleshoot as a user.

Businesses and government

Open Programs & Economy.

  • Applications: approve businesses, stocks, insurance and relief.
  • Businesses: view companies and migrate old business logins.
  • Bank Income: record how a bank earns or spends money.
  • Bank Owners: distribute declared bank profit or loss.
  • U.U.R. Government: taxes, assessments, departments and budgets.
  • Economy Charts: money supply, reserves and wealth concentration.

How business accounts work

  1. A player applies under Finance Services → My Businesses.
  2. An admin approves the company and its separate operating account.
  3. The owner selects the company before using payroll, invoices or reports.
  4. Staff use their own normal logins and receive only assigned permissions.
  5. Company money remains separate from personal money.

Stocks and company control

  • For a listed company, issued CSE shares equal legal ownership.
  • Available shares belong to nobody until issued or purchased.
  • Use Corporate Registry → Connect stock to company for older listings.
  • Use Apply shareholder control to grant operating authority to the largest holder.
  • The company—not the shareholder—owns the company bank account.

Casino safety setup

  1. Create a dedicated OC Casino House account.
  2. Create a separate U.U.R. Government Tax account.
  3. Set a reserve floor, maximum payout and daily-loss ceiling.
  4. Enable automatic closing when the reserve is threatened.
  5. Set wager, winnings and gross-gaming-revenue taxes.

Safe administration rules

  • Never use a customer account as a treasury account.
  • Use ledger adjustments rather than silently changing balances when possible.
  • Test new settings with small amounts.
  • Review the audit log after major actions.
  • Back up Firestore before migrations or large changes.
  • Firestore rules belong in Firebase, not GitHub Pages.
