Earnings intelligence

Built from the real transaction ledger — not self-reported filings. Income counts categorized earnings such as payroll, invoices/contracts, casino wins, interest, dividends and realized gains. Ordinary gifts and transfers are excluded by default; turn on the review option only when you intentionally want to inspect uncategorized incoming transfers. Suggested tax applies the published personal rate to that income. From here you can either issue a tax bill (the member pays it themselves from any bank page) or collect now (deducted immediately from their largest account into the treasury).
The ledger loads the newest 2,000 transactions. For a small server that's typically full history; if it ever isn't, the oldest activity falls outside the window.
