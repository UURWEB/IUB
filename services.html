BUSINESS PROFILE

How business accounts work

A business is a separate financial entity controlled from approved members’ normal personal logins.
Business accounts, access, ownership and records
  • You keep one normal login. Approved businesses appear in the Business Center and do not require shared passwords.
  • Business balances, transactions, payroll, invoices, taxes, insurance, loans and stock holdings stay separate from your personal accounts.
  • The legal owner appoints members and permissions such as accountant, payroll manager, CSE officer or administrator.
  • Choosing a business profile changes which business tools and accounts you are operating; it does not mix personal and business money.
  • Businesses may own shares in other businesses, receive dividends, cast shareholder votes and hold bank ownership stakes.
  • Parent companies may own private subsidiaries, controlled companies, joint ventures and indirect subsidiaries. For CSE-listed businesses, outstanding shares are the legal company-ownership units.
  • Legacy business logins can be migrated without replacing their account IDs or deleting their historical records.
