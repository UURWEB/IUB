Bank ownership and owner settlements

First assign ownership stakes. Later, declare a bank financial period. Positive results pay owners; negative results collect contributions and may create capital calls.

What this page does

Bank ownership configuration

Choose the bank treasury/capital account and the default share of declared results assigned to owners. Customer accounts remain separate.
Select a bank to review its current setup.

Add or update an owner

Owners may be players or approved businesses. Ownership is economic only and does not grant access to customer deposits.
Active ownership in one bank cannot exceed 100%. Stake participation can reduce how much of that owner’s percentage joins declared profits and losses.

Declare bank profit or loss

The owner pool is settled from or into the configured treasury account. Unpaid owner losses become capital-call liabilities.
Enter the period figures to preview the settlement.

Outstanding capital calls

When an ownership account cannot cover its full loss contribution, the unpaid portion remains here.
